    How to play

    Slide the cleat with your mouse, finger, or arrow keys. Hit near its edges to angle your shot.

    Defenders and walls lose health on impact. A goal moves you toward the next level. Miss the cleat and lose a life.

    Catch W for a wider cleat or C for curve. With curve active, hit off-center or sweep the cleat to bend the ball. Follow the blue trail.

    A rare invader appears at 2,000 points earned within each block of five levels. Hit them for +1 life before six crossings. One chance per block; no bonus goal or falling life pickup.

    From level 8, the keeper cannot be defeated. Every save speeds up the ball until you score or lose a life.
